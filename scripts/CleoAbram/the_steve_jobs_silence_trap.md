## SCRIPT (Teleprompter Clean)

[00:00] In 1997, a critic publicly insulted Steve Jobs to his face. Steve didn't argue. He stayed completely silent for fourteen agonizing seconds.

[00:08] The man told Steve he didn't know what he was talking about. Most people would have panicked or shouted back. But Steve took a slow sip of water and let the silence hang in the air.

[00:20] Here’s the brain glitch Steve exploited: humans are terrified of awkward silence. When you pause for more than three seconds, the other person’s brain enters a panic loop. They assume you know something they don't.

[00:32] In high-ticket sales, the person who speaks first after naming the price always loses. Amateurs rush to discount. Elite negotiators state the terms—and shut their mouths.

[00:44] Steve broke the silence, agreed with the critic, and walked away with total control. This is Day 8 of 30. Follow to master high-stakes psychology.
