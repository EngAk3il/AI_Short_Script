## SCRIPT (Teleprompter Clean)

[00:00] You think Ray-Ban and Oakley are fierce competitors. They’re not. They are owned by the exact same Italian company.

[00:07] It’s called Luxottica. They make eighty percent of all major sunglasses on Earth. Chanel, Prada, Versace, Armani? All made in the exact same Italian factories.

[00:19] You think you can boycott them by shopping at Sunglass Hut or LensCrafters? Luxottica owns those stores too. They control the factories, the brands, and the shelves.

[00:30] When Oakley tried to fight back and refused to sell, Luxottica kicked Oakley out of every Sunglass Hut. Oakley’s stock plunged forty percent—and Luxottica swallowed them whole.

[00:43] That three-hundred-dollar pair of sunglasses costs about five dollars to manufacture. This is Day X of Business Case Studies. Follow to see how global monopolies are actually built.
