## SCRIPT

[00:00] In school, if you get 50% on a test, you fail. But in venture capital, the math is completely inverted: you can fail 90% of the time and still make billions. Here's why this is huge if true.

[00:11] Most of our daily lives follow a normal bell curve. Human height, exam scores, commute times. But capital and technological innovation follow something called the Power Law.

[00:22] When an investor puts $1 million into a startup, the most they can ever lose is 1x their money. The downside is strictly capped at zero. But the upside? If that startup becomes the next Uber or Stripe, the return is 1,000x.

[00:34] Now, there’s a big catch. According to industry data, 65% of venture deals lose money, and Sequoia Capital admits that half of their investments make zero profit.

[00:44] But because of the Power Law, a single asymmetric outlier doesn't just cover the losses—it outperforms the entire rest of the fund combined.

[00:53] The lesson isn’t to avoid failure. It’s to find bets where your downside is capped, but your upside changes the world. I'm Engineer treader, follow for more systems that shape our future.
