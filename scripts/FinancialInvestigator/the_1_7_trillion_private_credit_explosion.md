## SCRIPT

[00:00] If you think JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs control corporate lending, you’re looking at an outdated map.

[00:09] Quietly behind the scenes, over $1.7 trillion has evacuated traditional Wall Street banks and vanished into the shadows.

[00:18] After the 2008 crash, strict federal regulations handcuffed commercial banks, making it nearly impossible for them to issue risky corporate loans.

[00:28] In stepped shadow giants like Apollo and Blackstone. Instead of taking retail deposits, they locked up trillions from pension funds and sovereign wealth into unregulated private credit.

[00:39] The catch? These shadow lenders have no FDIC insurance and zero mark-to-market transparency. They price loans behind closed doors at whatever they want.

[00:49] When interest rates bite, banks won’t be the ones collapsing. It’s your retirement fund holding the hidden debt. Follow for the real mechanics of global capital.
