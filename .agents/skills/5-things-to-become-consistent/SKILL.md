# 5 Things To Do To Become Consistent In Any Work

This skill is designed to guide the creation of a 5-point script about building consistency in life or work.

## Core Lessons (The 5 Things)
1. **The 'One Minute' Rule:** Start by committing just 60 seconds a day. The hardest part is starting.
2. **Design the Environment (Foot fall hook):** Make the good habit the easiest path. If you want to read, put the book on your pillow. Reduce friction.
3. **Track the Micro-Wins (Data hook):** Don't measure the end goal, measure the daily input. A simple calendar cross-off builds powerful momentum.
4. **The 'Cost of Failure' Mindset:** Accept that missing one day is a slip, but missing two days is the start of a new, bad habit. The cost of failure is less if you bounce back instantly.
5. **Attach to an Existing Habit (Anchor):** Don't create a new time slot. Attach the new habit to something you already do every day (e.g., after brushing teeth).

## Application for Creators

### ThinkSchool_Hindi Tone
- Focus on the *business logic* of consistency.
- Use terms like `cost of failure is less`, `foot fall`, and `D2C (Direct to Consistency)`.
- Ask the entrepreneur viewer: What is their `pehla step`?

### NiharikaChoudhary Tone
- Focus on the *injustice* we do to our own potential by being inconsistent.
- Ask: `Aakhir kya hua` that we stopped fighting for our goals?
- Use a passionate, civic duty tone. Ask the viewer: `Aap kya sochte hain?`
