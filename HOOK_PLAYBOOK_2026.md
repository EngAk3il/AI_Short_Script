## 👑 The 7 Proven Viral Hook Archetypes for Finance & Trading

Every new script MUST select and specify one of these 7 tested archetypes:

---

### Archetype 1: The Tactile / Physical Prop Hook (Cleo Abram Model)
- **Psychological Trigger:** Visual Curiosity Gap. The brain sees an unfamiliar physical object or familiar object in an unfamiliar context and stays to see what it is.
- **Formula:** [Physical Prop Held to Camera] + [Impossible Contradiction / Scale Shift]
- **Cleo Abram Reference:** *"This tiny dot is a robot. Here it is on a penny. But the way it moves is crazy."*
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *(Holding up an iPhone screen showing $0.00 fee):* *"Notice how your stock app charges you zero dollars to trade? That’s not a feature. In 12 milliseconds, an algorithm just robbed you."*
  - *(Holding a black card):* *"This piece of plastic costs $695 a year. But it secretly pays me $4,000—if you know the loophole."*

---

### Archetype 2: The Ego Gut-Punch / Human Guilt Hook (Alex Hormozi Model)
- **Psychological Trigger:** Cognitive Dissonance & Self-Image Threat. Hits the viewer with their own embarrassing subconscious hypocrisy or fatal blind spot.
- **Formula:** [Thing You Did to Win] + [Brutal Truth That It Destroys You]
- **Alex Hormozi Reference:** *"You quit your job because you don't want to work as hard... but as soon as you quit, you realize you're working twice as hard for half the money."*
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"You just made $3,000 before lunch. You feel like a genius. And that exact feeling is why your account will be empty by Friday."*
  - *"You worked 80 hours this week to save $5,000. And the government just took $2,000 of it before you even touched your bank app."*

---

### Archetype 3: The Negative Warning / Loss-Aversion Hook
- **Psychological Trigger:** Negativity Bias (Kahneman & Tversky). Humans are **2.5x more motivated** to prevent losing $1,000 than to make $1,000.
- **Formula:** Stop [Common Action] + [The Catastrophic Reason Why]
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"Stop putting your stop-loss below obvious support. An institutional algorithm is literally watching that exact price."*
  - *"Never accept a promotion until you look at this tax bracket chart. You might actually take home less cash."*

---

### Archetype 4: The Shocking Juxtaposition / Paradox Hook
- **Psychological Trigger:** Radical Incongruity. Two completely incompatible facts placed in the same sentence break the brain's prediction model.
- **Formula:** [Massive Global Catastrophe / Fortune] + [Completely Absurd / Underdog Reality]
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"In five minutes, one trillion dollars vanished from Wall Street. It wasn't a Russian cyberattack—it was a 31-year-old sitting in his parents' bedroom in London wearing sweatpants."*
  - *"This 24-year-old made $18 million in 48 hours. And he did it by buying crude oil that was priced at negative $37 a barrel."*

---

### Archetype 5: The "Undercover / Illicit Secret" Hook (Financial Investigator Model)
- **Psychological Trigger:** Fear of Being Exploited / Insider Knowledge. Positions the viewer as the victim of a covert institutional game.
- **Formula:** [Harmless Everyday Activity] + [Dark Reality: You Are Being Exploited / Used]
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"If you have a 401(k) or a retirement fund, you are currently an undercover loan shark. And nobody bothered to tell you."*
  - *"In 2018, Elon Musk paid zero dollars in federal income tax. In 2007, Jeff Bezos paid zero dollars. Not a single law was broken."*

---

### Archetype 6: The Universal Trader Agony Hook (Deep Relatability)
- **Psychological Trigger:** Shared Trauma. Describes a hyperspecific, painful experience that 99% of the niche has felt.
- **Formula:** [Hyperspecific Agonizing Experience] + [The Shocking Real Cause]
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"Ever had your stop-loss get hit by literally ONE cent... kick you out... and then shoot straight to the moon without you?"*
  - *"Ever had a green trade up $800, refuse to take profit, and watch it bleed into a $1,200 loss? Here’s the chemical glitch that caused it."*

---

### Archetype 7: The Awkward Story / Dramatic Freeze Hook
- **Psychological Trigger:** Narrative Tension & Voyeurism. Drops the viewer directly into a high-stakes room during an uncomfortable confrontation.
- **Formula:** [Moment of Public Attack / High Stakes] + [Uncanny, Unexpected Reaction]
- **Finance/Trading Application:**
  - *"In 1997, a critic publicly insulted Steve Jobs to his face. Steve didn't argue. He stayed completely silent for fourteen agonizing seconds."*
  - *"When the bank teller asked a billionaire why he was withdrawing $10 million in cash, he answered with one sentence that terrified the manager."*
