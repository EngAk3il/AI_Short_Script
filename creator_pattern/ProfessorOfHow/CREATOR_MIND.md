# ProfessorOfHow DNA

**Category:** 3D Explainer / Scientific Mechanisms

### Core Mechanics
- Starts directly with a high-impact, curiosity-driven hook (VIRAL_FACT or NEWS_ANCHOR style).
- Narrative relies heavily on "3D Explainer" visual language, meaning the script is designed to be paired with 3D anatomical, mechanical, or structural animations.
- Maintains a rapid, dense pacing (~4.0 - 4.5 WPS).
- Concludes with a punchline, a thought-provoking open question, or a direct call-to-action regarding a course/product.

### Voice & Vocabulary
- **Signature Tics:** Uses straightforward Hindi explanations (e.g., "यह हादसा", "है कि", "वो सब").
- **Required Framing:** Explains the "HOW" behind complex phenomena (e.g., diseases, accidents, economic scenarios).
- **Never Use:** Generic news intros like "Aaj hum baat karenge", "Dosto", or "Bhai", and the cliché phrase "3D animation se detail mein samajhte hain".
