## TOP 10 HOOK FORMULAS (Ranked by Engagement)

| # | Formula | Fill-in-the-blank |
|---|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | The "Huge If True" Scale | "If this works, it fundamentally changes how [global system] works forever. Here's what's actually happening." |
| 2 | The Science-Fiction Reality | "This sounds like something straight out of science fiction, but [engineers / researchers] just proved it in real life." |
| 3 | The Fear Reframe | "Everyone is terrified that [trend / technology] is about to ruin [X]. But if you look at the mechanics, the real story is the exact opposite." |
| 4 | The Tactile Anchor | "Look at this [tiny object / line of code / balance sheet]. Right now, this single mechanism is quietly deciding the future of a [trillion-dollar industry]." |
| 5 | The Hidden Bottleneck | "You've probably heard that [problem] is caused by [common scapegoat]. But the real bottleneck is something nobody is talking about." |
| 6 | The Question of Wonder | "Why did [scientists / builders] spend [huge sum / years] trying to solve something that seems completely impossible?" |
| 7 | The Counter-Intuitive Pivot | "Most people think [X] is doomed. But behind the scenes, a quiet breakthrough just changed the math." |
| 8 | The 3-Second Paradox | "We've been told for decades that [X] was impossible. Last week, that rule broke." |
| 9 | The High-Stakes Race | "Right now, there's a silent race between [two forces / nations / systems] that will determine how you live by 2030." |
| 10 | The Direct Stakes ("Why it matters") | "This might look like boring [infrastructure / regulation / finance], but it directly impacts the price of everything you buy." |

## 5-PHASE 60-SECOND ARC

```
00:00 - 00:08 | STOP  → The "Huge If True" Wonder or Paradox Hook
00:09 - 00:20 | TRAP  → Establish the high stakes & the common misconception
00:21 - 00:38 | BUILD → The First-Principles Mechanical Breakdown (Step-by-step)
00:39 - 00:48 | TWIST → The Skeptic Pivot ("The catch") & The Real Solution
00:49 - 00:60 | CLOSE → The Macroscopic Horizon & Earned Optimism CTA
```

---

## FORBIDDEN PATTERNS ❌
- Cynical doomerism ("We are all doomed")
- Snarky or condescending attacks on individuals
- Unexplained technical or financial acronyms
- Overpromising magic without showing the engineering/economic trade-off
- Generic clickbait without an immediate, satisfying payoff
